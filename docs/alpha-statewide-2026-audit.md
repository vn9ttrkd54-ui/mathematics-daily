## Label and difficulty calibration

No new topic or method labels are introduced. All topics/methods are tested against the pre-existing public vocabulary. Methods are optional: the waveform modeling, basic growth, finite-difference, and circle-sector questions do not receive an unrelated generic method. In particular, no integral trick is labeled Integral Properties.

One star covers direct recall or one-step algebra; two stars covers routine multi-step algebra/identities; three stars covers longer domain, conic, number-theory, or geometric reasoning. Four stars is reserved for the telescoping trig product, parametric minimality, shifted parity, increasing inverse-function intersection, reflected-kite maximum, complex-plane region, and coefficient-counting problems. These decisions follow the existing Alpha scale, independently of contest title or question position.
